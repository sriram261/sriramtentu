Case study: Bob's Burger Palace, a VR world for stuttering therapy

Oct 15, 2026 · Case study

The problem

The hardest speaking situations for a person who stutters are ordinary ones: ordering food, asking a stranger a question, small talk with a cashier. You cannot rehearse real life in a clinic room. But you can build a place that feels real enough to practice in, and safe enough to fail in.

What I built

Bob's Burger Palace is a full 3D restaurant environment built for stuttering therapy and research:

  • Immersive Unity 3D world you walk into with an HTC Vive or Oculus Quest
  • Characters built with Reallusion Character Creator 4, animated with Mixamo
  • Speaking scenarios based on everyday interactions, ordering, asking, responding
  • Built as my master's thesis research at Oklahoma State University
Unity 3DC#VRMixamoReallusion

The result

Participants showed a 45% improvement in speech fluency. The work became my master's thesis (ProQuest, 2024), won the Best Outstanding Poster Award at IEEE SeGAH 2024 in Funchal, Portugal, and led to publications with Springer and talks at the ASHA Convention in 2024 and 2025.
