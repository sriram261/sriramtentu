The India move: Oklahoma to Kaikaluru

Oct 15, 2026 · 2 min read

In June 2027, I move home. Oklahoma to Kaikaluru, Andhra Pradesh. Completely.

People ask why I would leave a US tech career. Honest answer: I want to build something of my own, where it matters most to me.

This is not a someday plan. The base is already built:

  • TenTwo Technologies Private Limited is registered in India, and its internship program has already run a cohort
  • Tuberose & Co. sells on Amazon India
  • The Smiles initiative has launched
  • RamTech Solutions LLC keeps serving US clients from Oklahoma, and it is not going anywhere

Between now and June 2027, the work does not slow down. I am shipping client platforms, growing both companies, and writing it down as I go. When I land in Kaikaluru, I am not starting from zero. I am arriving with running businesses, real clients, and a plan measured in years, not months.

If you are building something in Andhra Pradesh, or you need a website or a platform built before I go, my door is open on both sides of the ocean.
