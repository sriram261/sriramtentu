Case study: replacing spreadsheets with a platform for 400+ clinics

Oct 15, 2026 · Case study

The problem

Argencis is a durable medical equipment (DME) provider. Its order tracking ran on a manual Google Sheets workflow, shared across more than 400 referral sources. Every order meant rows typed by hand, status updates chased by phone, and no single place where anyone could answer a simple question: where is this order?

Spreadsheets are fine until they are the system. At 400+ referral sources, they were the bottleneck.

What I built

A HIPAA-aware order tracking platform, built from scratch and running in production:

  • Order tracking dashboard with live status for every order
  • CSV ingestion from Brightree, the system referral sources already use
  • Masking rules so sensitive patient data only shows where it should
  • SMS notifications as orders move
  • Sign-in and access control with AWS Cognito

The result

The manual spreadsheet workflow is gone. The platform is the system of record for 400+ clinics and referral sources, and it is still growing: Phase 2 adds a physician filter and an advanced analytics dashboard.
