Case study: FluentLoop, speech practice that lives in a browser

Oct 15, 2026 · Case study

The problem

People who stutter improve with practice, but the good tools live in therapy rooms and research labs: scheduled, supervised, and hard to get to. Practice needs to happen between sessions, at home, on a normal day. A VR headset in a lab does not solve that. A browser tab does.

What I built

FluentLoop takes what my VR research taught me and puts it in a web app any student can open:

  • Speech exercises you run right in the browser, no install, no headset
  • Whisper integration that listens to what you actually said, scores it, and shows you the gap
  • Animated results that make progress visible instead of abstract
  • A level-based progression system that gives practice a path
  • A companion Flutter app alongside the web platform

The result

FluentLoop is live at fluentloopai.com. It stopped being a demo the day a professor asked to use it with his own students. Version 2 is in progress: real Whisper integration end to end, user accounts, session history, and a new progression system.
